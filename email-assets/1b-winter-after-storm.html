After the storm
A 5-minute
home check.

Hi $[LF:FIRSTNAME]$, winter storms hide damage until it turns into a leak. Once it is safe to go outside, take five minutes:

1
 Walk around the house and look up: gutters, shingles, siding and trim.
2
 Check inside ceilings and the tops of exterior walls for new stains.
3
 Clear ice and snow from deck stairs, railings and walkways.
4
 Check your basement for water near the foundation and around pipes.
5
 Photograph any damage before you clean up, in case you file an insurance claim.
Spotted damage? Call us and we will come take a look. The sooner it is fixed, the less it costs.
