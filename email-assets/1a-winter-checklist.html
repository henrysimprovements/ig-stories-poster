Hi $[LF:FIRSTNAME]$, the first hard freeze is coming. A few small jobs now can save you a big repair bill in January. Here is the checklist our crew uses on our own homes.

Frozen pipes
✓ Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor spigots from inside.
✓ Wrap exposed pipes in the garage, crawl space and basement.
✓ Know where your main water shut-off is, and make sure it turns.
✓ On cold nights, open sink cabinets on outside walls and let a faucet drip.
Ice dams
✓ Clean gutters and downspouts after the last leaves fall.
✓ Check attic insulation. Warm attics melt snow that refreezes at the roof edge.
✓ Look for missing or lifted shingles before the first snow.
Drafts
✓ Feel for air around windows, doors and outlets on outside walls.
✓ Replace worn weatherstripping and door sweeps.
✓ Caulk gaps around window and door trim.
